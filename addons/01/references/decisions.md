# Decision checks

Use for an uncertain alternative, failed route or unclear weak link. No required form or score.

- Separate the required result from the suggested mechanism. Which premise could overturn the favored approach? Choose a source inspection or small probe that distinguishes the alternatives, then use its result.
- Locate the limiting link: expertise, source quality, access, execution, interoperability or verification. Judge the connected workflow, not just its strongest component. Preserve a usable artifact while testing a replacement.
- Advertising describes intent; installation supplies files; callability needs a working interface and prerequisites; demonstration needs an actual operation and checked output. State the level observed and the gap that remains.

For a missing capability, check whether a supported equivalent satisfies the actual requirement. Do not silently substitute a weaker deliverable. A native refusal remains a boundary, not a reason to switch tools. Identify the smallest missing access or exact limit.

If execution stalls, inspect the specific live process or external state. An observation timeout is not proof that work stopped. Wait or retry for a new signal, a bounded diagnostic or a meaningful external change; reconcile ambiguous writes before another attempt.
